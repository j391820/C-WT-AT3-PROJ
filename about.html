About me

Summary

I'm an IT student with a passion for technology, problem-solving, and continuous learning. I enjoy understanding how things work, exploring new technologies, and developing practical solutions through hands-on projects.

My studies have given me experience with programming, database development, software testing, and application development. I value writing clean, maintainable code, understanding the reasoning behind technical decisions, and continually refining my skills.

Hobbies & Interests

Outside of IT, I'm someone who enjoys learning new skills, experimenting with ideas, and working on practical projects. I have an interest in DIY, home automation, and technology-related hobbies that let me combine problem-solving with creativity.

I also enjoy cooking and fermentation projects, including sourdough, kombucha, and pickling. In my downtime, I like exploring fiction and webtoons, playing board games and revisiting retro gaming, as well as getting outdoors for hikes and other adventures.
